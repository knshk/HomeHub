Four ways the desktop view could work

The launcher was built phone-first, so on a 1850px screen it read as a narrow column of small tiles in a lot of empty room — disconnected and poorly placed, as you put it. Below are four directions, from a light touch to a genuine desktop composition, plus the bottom bar you couldn't judge unseen. Palette and theme switch all of them.

Already fixed and live from your last round — just refresh:

  • Two-up tiles on the phone. The 190px minimum silently collapsed to one column at 360px; phone widths now use an explicit 2-col.
  • Chat history opens under the header, beside the chip you tapped — no more scrolling to the end of a long chat to find it.
  • Composer starts at one line, grows to four, scrolls after that, and snaps back to one line on send. It also floats clear of the home indicator.

My recommendation

D2 now, D3 after launch. The left rail fixes the actual complaint — a cut-off tab rail and a layout that ignores the width — for moderate effort, and it's the shell D3's cards would later drop into. D4's home composer is cheap and could be added to either as the hero. On the phone I'd take the bottom bar: it costs little and puts Chat one thumb-tap away from anywhere.
